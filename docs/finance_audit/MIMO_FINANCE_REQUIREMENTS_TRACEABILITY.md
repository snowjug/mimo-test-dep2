## 2. Requirements Risk Classification

- **CRITICAL FINANCIAL ACCURACY (Immediate Blockers to Financial Reporting):**
  - **A3 & B1:** Inception date correction (Jan 2026) and elimination of fake `HISTORICAL_MONTHS`.
  - **B2 & F2:** Separation of Gross Collections from Bank Settlements and integration of Cashfree UTRs.
  - **E2:** Elimination of wallet top-up vs. purchase double-counting.
  - **A4:** Kiosk revenue multi-job attribution correction.
- **OPERATIONAL INTEGRITY & CONTROL:**
  - **C1 & C3:** Unrestricted pending refund visibility and dual-control bulk approvals.
  - **G2:** Migration of expenses from transient React state to persistent, auditable datastore.
  - **A2:** Role segregation and non-admin Finance Manager permissions.
- **ENHANCEMENT & REFACTORING:**
  - **D1 & D2:** Coupon redemption ledger and privacy masking.
  - **H1 & H2:** Customer Analytics redesign and performance indexing.
  - **I1:** Clean removal of Team Chat from Finance.
  - **J1:** Production dark mode styling.
