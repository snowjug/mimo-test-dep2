Questions. Quick answers.

Which files can I print?

PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, text files, JPG and PNG. PDFs give the most faithful result; other formats are converted to PDF before printing.

How long is my print code valid?

Your 4-digit code is valid for 24 hours. If you don't collect your prints within that window, your payment is refunded automatically.

How can I pay?

All major UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit and credit cards, and net banking. Payments are processed securely by Cashfree.

Is my document private?

Files are encrypted in transit and permanently deleted from our servers right after printing. We never store, read or share your documents.
