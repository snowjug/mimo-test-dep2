Our Story

Built by students.
For students.

MIMO started with a simple observation: printing on campus shouldn't be this difficult.

As students, we experienced the long queues, last-minute rushes, and inconvenience of getting documents printed when we needed them most.

So we decided to build a better way. What started as an idea became MIMO — a self-service printing kiosk designed to make campus printing simple, quick, and accessible.

We built, tested, and improved MIMO around real student needs — learning from every print, every user, and every challenge along the way.

Today, we're continuing to build MIMO into more than just a printing kiosk — a smarter way to access everyday campus services.

Privacy

Printed.
Then deleted.

Files are encrypted in transit and permanently deleted from our servers right after printing. We never store, read or share your documents.

Questions. Quick answers.

Which files can I print?

PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, text files, JPG and PNG. PDFs give the most faithful result; other formats are converted to PDF before printing.

How long is my print code valid?

Your 4-digit code is valid for 24 hours. If you don't collect your prints within that window, your payment is refunded automatically.

How can I pay?

All major UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit and credit cards, and net banking. Payments are processed securely by Cashfree.

Is my document private?

Files are encrypted in transit and permanently deleted from our servers right after printing. We never store, read or share your documents.
